# 🚀 JobMate

### Quote smarter. Know your profit.

JobMate is a simple, mobile-friendly quoting and profit-management app designed to help tradies create professional quotes, manage customers, and keep track of job profitability.

## 🌐 Launch JobMate

**[👉 OPEN JOBMATE](https://communityasset.github.io/jobmate/)**

Use the live app above to open the latest version of JobMate.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🧾 Create professional quotes
- 👥 Manage customers
- 💰 Calculate labour, materials, equipment and travel costs
- 📈 Track estimated profit
- 🧮 Calculate GST
- 📋 Manage quote statuses
- 💾 Save and manage quote history
- 🖨️ Print or save professional quotes as PDF
- 📤 Back up and restore your JobMate data
- 📱 Mobile-friendly design

---

## 🔄 Quote Workflow

JobMate lets you track quotes through a simple workflow:

**Draft → Sent → Accepted → Paid**

Quotes can also be marked as **Declined**.

---

## 💼 Business Setup

Set up your business details once and JobMate can use them throughout your quotes, including:

- Business name
- ABN
- Phone
- Email
- Business address
- Default quote terms

---

## 📊 Built for Tradies

JobMate is designed around the information that matters when preparing a quote:

- Labour
- Materials
- Equipment
- Travel / fuel
- Other costs
- Profit margin
- GST
- Customer details
- Job details

The goal is simple:

> **Know what the job costs. Know what you need to charge. Know your profit.**
